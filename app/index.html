didahSDR - Quick Guide & Shortcuts

UI overall principles

didahSDR focuses on using the mouse to navigate the waterfall and quickly select frequencies.

IQ sources

Open the Source window to switch between VA2GKA Replay (local 96 kHz recording) and a live KiwiSDR (12 kHz IQ). Paste the receiver web address, for example http://f4kiy.ddns.net:8073. On live Kiwi the waterfall is a 12 kHz zoom; tuning past the edges or Shift+wheel / ruler-drag slides the Kiwi DDC.

Config

Open Config for waterfall levels, WF Speed (1x–8x STFT overlap), CW BFO/BW, iambic A/B, and USB/LSB low/high (default 200–2700 Hz). QRSS (in the WATERFALL section) switches the FFT to a Blackman window and averages successive spectra so slow Morse stands out; WF Speed then sets how long each column integrates.

Mouse behaviours

  • Mouse Wheel: Move the VFO up and down according to the frequency step selected.
  • CTRL + Mouse Wheel / CTRL + Left Drag: Zoom the waterfall, centred on the cursor.
  • SHIFT + Mouse Wheel / SHIFT + Left Drag: Pan the waterfall up and down (on live Kiwi this slides the 12 kHz DDC).
  • CTRL + SHIFT + Wheel / Drag: Step CW bandwidth, or USB/LSB High when in SSB.
  • Left Click on Waterfall: Move the VFO to the selected frequency (hold and drag to track).
  • Left Click on the Ruler: Drag to pan the waterfall up and down.

Local CW transmit

CW mode only, local audio and waterfall — nothing is sent to the server yet. TRANSMIT arms the typeahead line (bright red) without muting RX. Typing, or clicking TRANSMIT with text already in the box, keys a sidetone at the CW BFO and paints the VFO on the waterfall. The buffer grows if you type faster than Morse; when it empties, RX returns after one extra letter-space (no RX between letters). Click TRANSMIT again to disarm; remaining text stays in the box. F8/F9 are an iambic paddle (mode A/B in Config, B default); F4 is a straight key. Paddles work even when TRANSMIT is not armed.

Analog Mechanical Drum Dial (VFO)

  • Hover + Mouse Wheel: Roll any individual digit drum up or down.
  • Left Click on a digit: Selects the digit cursor. Use Arrow Up/Down to spin the drum or type digits 0-9.
  • Right Click on a digit: Zeroes out all digits to the right (convenient rounding tool).
