didahSDR - Quick Guide & Shortcuts

UI overall principles

didahSDR focuses on using the mouse to navigate the waterfall and quickly select frequencies.

Mouse behaviours

  • Mouse Wheel: Move the VFO / cursor up and down on the Waterfall according to the frequency step selected.
  • CTRL + Mouse Wheel / CTRL + Left Drag: Zoom the waterfall, centred on the cursor.
  • SHIFT + Mouse Wheel / SHIFT + Left Drag: Pan the waterfall up and down.
  • CTRL + SHIFT + Wheel / Left Drag: Step CW bandwidth, or USB/LSB High when in SSB.
  • Left Click on Waterfall: Move the VFO to the selected frequency (hold and drag to track).
  • Drag on the Ruler: Drag to pan the waterfall up and down.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Toggle PTT (CW only, including the CW text box)

F8
CW dit paddle (PTT On, local sidetone)
F9
CW dah paddle (iambic squeeze with F8; PTT On)
F4
CW straight key (PTT On, tone while held)

Analog Mechanical Drum Dial (VFO)

  • Hover + Mouse Wheel: Roll any individual digit drum up or down.
  • Left Click on a digit: Selects the digit cursor. Use Arrow Up/Down to spin the drum or type digits 0-9.
  • Right Click on a digit: Zeroes out all digits to the right (convenient rounding tool).

Local CW transmit

CW mode only, by typing text or using F8/F9/F4 as a Iambic paddle or straight key. Use PTT On to arms/stop the transmission.
